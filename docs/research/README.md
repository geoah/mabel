# Research

Background research feeding the mabel proposal. Files are numbered in the
order they were produced.

- [001-hearsay-digest.md](001-hearsay-digest.md): what hearsay does, its
  domain model and CLI, the hearsay-to-mabel mapping, and the gaps our
  simplifications open (flags W, A, P, L, R, D).
- [002-iroh-research.md](002-iroh-research.md): Iroh 1.0.3 APIs, crate
  versions, protocol pattern recommendation, addressing, and test setups.
- [003-phase4-verification.md](003-phase4-verification.md): every acceptance
  criterion of tickets 001 to 022 checked against the artifact it names, with
  the 13 failures, the 5 criteria docker and screenshots make unverifiable, and
  the global gate results.
